Project Overview

This project analyzes an online retail dataset containing more than 500,000 transaction records. The objective was to transform raw transactional data into an interactive Power BI dashboard that helps stakeholders understand sales performance, customer behavior, product performance and returns.

The project demonstrates an end-to-end data analytics workflow, from data cleaning and transformation to visualization, business analysis and actionable recommendations.

Business Objectives

  • Measure overall sales and revenue performance.
  • Analyze sales trends across time.
  • Identify high-performing products.
  • Understand customer purchasing behavior.
  • Analyze returned transactions.
  • Identify opportunities for business growth.

Data Analytics Process

1. Data Cleaning

The raw dataset required extensive preparation before analysis. Missing values, duplicate records, invalid prices and transaction-related inconsistencies were reviewed and handled.

2. Data Transformation

Power Query was used to transform the dataset and create useful analytical fields including date attributes, transaction classifications and calculated business measures.

3. Data Modeling

The cleaned data was structured to support efficient analysis and meaningful relationships between relevant business entities.
